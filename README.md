### USDT from one network to another

To move it from TRON to Ethereum without an account, VertEXchange.pro lets you [swap USDT TRC20 to ERC20](https://vertexchange.pro/en/exchange/usdt-trc20-to-usdt-erc20) straight to your own address; THORChain does the same for stablecoins on the chains it supports, from your own wallet.

## Check a service yourself

1. Open its terms and search for "KYC", "AML", "source of funds" and "refund". A line saying ID "may be requested" means it can be requested from you.
2. Find out who holds the deposit during the swap. If it lands on the service's wallet, the service can stop it.
3. Read what happens to a flagged deposit: sent back to you, held until you show ID, or kept.
4. Give a refund address when the form asks. Without one, a failed swap turns into a support ticket.
5. Look the service up on kycnot.me and in recent BitcoinTalk and Reddit threads before sending a large amount.
6. Ignore anyone who asks for an "unlock fee" to release a swap. That is a scam, every time.

## FAQ

### Which crypto exchanges never ask for KYC?

In this index: VertEXchange.pro, Unstoppable Wallet, THORChain. Among P2P and atomic swap projects, all four never ask: eigenwallet, Bisq, Haveno, RetoSwap.

### Can an instant exchange freeze my crypto?

If it receives your deposit on its own wallet, yes. In this index the terms of WizardSwap, Exolix, ChangeNOW, FixedFloat, Godex, Changelly allow freezing funds. A service that never holds your coins has nothing to freeze.

### How do I buy Monero without KYC?

Swap a coin you already have for XMR on a service that never asks for ID. On VertEXchange.pro you can [exchange USDT TRC20 for Monero](https://vertexchange.pro/en/exchange/usdt-trc20-to-xmr) or bitcoin for XMR without an account; eigenwallet and RetoSwap take longer but need no service in the middle.

### Is it legal to swap crypto without KYC?

In most countries, swapping one coin for another without an account is legal. Taxes on your gains apply either way.

### How often is this index updated?

Every month. Spotted a mistake or had a swap frozen? [Open an issue](../../issues/new).
